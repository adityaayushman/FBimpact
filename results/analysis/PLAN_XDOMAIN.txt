PRE-REGISTRATION ADDENDUM: HELD-OUT-DOMAIN EVALUATION
Written 2026-10-12, before any run of the protocols below. Do not edit after the
first run; add dated addenda instead. This is a separate document from PLAN.txt
and does not change any rule in it.

QUESTION
Do the results on the combined benchmark survive a change of place and camera?
The in-domain folds mix clips from the same rooms and cameras (and, where actors
are unlabelled, probably the same people) across train and test. A held-out
domain removes that.

PROTOCOLS (one split each; configs/xd_<protocol>_<arm>.yaml)
  urfd_to_le2i            train: UR Fall                       test: all Le2i
  le2i_to_urfd            train: Le2i                          test: all UR Fall
  leave_coffee_room_out   train: UR Fall + Le2i Home           test: Le2i Coffee room
  leave_home_out          train: UR Fall + Le2i Coffee room    test: Le2i Home
Validation subjects are drawn from the training side only and fix the decision
threshold, so the test domain never influences any choice.

ARMS
  ctrl    combined_xyv      (x, y, vx, vy) features, binary head
  phases  combined_phases   full features + four-phase head + consistency
Seeds 1, 2, 3 for every cell (24 runs). Seed 0 is not used.

REPORTED, FOR EVERY CELL
  recall (pooled: warned / falls), absolute false-alarm count on the held-out
  domain's normal clips, negative minutes (so the rate is interpretable),
  mean lead time, frame AUC.
Per protocol: the phases-minus-ctrl difference in false alarms and in recall,
seed-averaged, with a paired clip-level bootstrap interval (same procedure as
scripts/analyze_arms.py).
Per arm: the DOMAIN GAP, that is held-out recall and false alarms against the
in-domain fold results of the same arm.

WHAT THIS DOCUMENT CAN CLAIM
Nothing about the four-phase hypothesis. That is decided by PLAN.txt alone. These
runs can only support statements of the form "the in-domain result did / did not
carry over to a held-out domain" and measure the size of the domain gap. If the
PLAN.txt confirmation fails, these runs are reported as a description of
generalisation, not as evidence for the phases arm.

LIMITS TO STATE WITH ANY RESULT
Both datasets are acted falls by young volunteers. Le2i's four scenes are three
rooms and one camera each; two of them are the held-out scenes here. Le2i has no
actor labels, so a held-out scene removes the room and camera but not necessarily
the people. The held-out domains are small (see the clip counts printed by the
runner), and one false alarm is a large fraction of any count, so a difference of
a few alarms is not distinguishable from noise.

Consequences, stated now so they cannot be discovered afterwards:
  - One false alarm is roughly 7-15 per hour on these test sets. False-alarm
    differences of a few counts will not be called a result.
  - urfd_to_le2i trains on only 22 falls; a poor outcome there says as much about
    training-set size as about domain shift.
  - Recall (over 30-99 falls) is the better-powered quantity in this document.
